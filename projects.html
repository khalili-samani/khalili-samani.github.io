Luxury Fashion Outlet Sales Analysis

Built a five-page Power BI dashboard for a fictional luxury retail outlet to help store and regional managers assess revenue quality, profitability, promotions, product mix, staff performance and customer contact capture. I cleaned and standardised 6,287 transaction rows in Power Query, retained 6,200 unique normalised transactions, designed a star-schema model and created DAX measures for sales, gross profit, margin, average order value, returns, discounts and email capture.

The dashboard reported $2.369 million in net sales, $917,029 in gross profit and a 38.71% gross margin. The analysis identified outerwear as a material revenue category with the lowest category margin, while accessories delivered the highest margin but the lowest sales, giving management clear areas for pricing, promotion and product-placement review. The dataset and business scenario are synthetic and intended for portfolio demonstration.

Technologies: Power BI Desktop, Power Query (M), DAX

Skills: Data cleaning, dimensional modelling, KPI design, dashboard development, business analysis, data visualisation, stakeholder communication

Australian NEM Price Forecasting

Developed an end-to-end forecasting platform to predict Australian National Electricity Market dispatch prices 30 minutes ahead using AEMO market data and weather observations. The workflow covers data ingestion, MySQL storage, cleaning, leakage-safe feature engineering, chronological model validation and delivery through an interactive Streamlit dashboard with scenario analysis and market-history views.

The pipeline processed 129,495 market records and trained an XGBoost regressor on more than 90,000 observations. On the hold-out test set, the model achieved a mean absolute error of $19.02/MWh, RMSE of $33.43/MWh and R² of 0.5085, demonstrating useful short-term signal while clearly documenting the difficulty of forecasting extreme price spikes.

Technologies: Python, SQL, MySQL, Pandas, SQLAlchemy, XGBoost, Streamlit

Skills: Time-series forecasting, ETL, feature engineering, model evaluation, dashboard development, analytical reporting

ASX Equity Intelligence Agent

Designed a multi-agent decision-support workflow that automates daily ASX equity analysis across market-data collection, next-day return forecasting, portfolio risk assessment, recommendation generation and executive-style market reporting. Five specialised agents share structured state through LangGraph, while the language model is restricted to communicating supplied analytical outputs rather than creating forecasts or trading signals.

A documented pipeline run loaded 2,540 OHLCV records for 10 ASX-listed companies, generated 10 Random Forest forecasts using walk-forward validation, assessed Value at Risk, volatility, beta, drawdown and sector concentration, and produced a daily market brief. This project demonstrates how machine learning, risk analytics and generative AI can be combined with explicit controls for explainability and analytical integrity.

Technologies: Python, DuckDB, Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, LangGraph, Gemini API

Skills: Financial analytics, machine learning, risk analysis, workflow orchestration, prompt design, automated reporting

Australian Energy Market Analysis Pipeline

Created a repeatable ETL and reporting pipeline that converts raw Australian Energy Market Operator data into analysis-ready tables, reusable SQL views and automated visual reports. The solution validates and standardises five-minute price and demand data, loads it into a MySQL fact table, and separates reporting logic from presentation through dedicated daily and regional summary views.

The analysis compared NSW, Queensland and Victoria across average price, demand, volatility and negative-price frequency. March 2026 results showed Victoria had the highest price volatility and widest price distribution, while NSW generally recorded the highest average prices. Four automated charts translate the operational records into business-readable regional insights.

Technologies: Python, SQL, MySQL, Pandas, SQLAlchemy, PyMySQL, Matplotlib

Skills: ETL development, data validation, relational modelling, SQL reporting, market analysis, automated visualisation

Sector-Based Stock Market Analysis

Built a reusable financial analytics pipeline to compare risk and return across 12 large US companies in Technology, Finance, Energy and Healthcare. Historical price data is cleaned and loaded into a dimensional MySQL warehouse, then transformed through three analytical SQL views that calculate daily returns, company summaries and sector-level performance.

The reporting layer showed Nvidia had both the highest average daily return and highest volatility, while Healthcare companies clustered in the lower-risk, lower-return range. The project demonstrates practical use of SQL window functions, dimensional modelling and automated visualisation to communicate risk-return trade-offs rather than presenting raw market data alone.

Technologies: Python, SQL, MySQL, Pandas, SQLAlchemy, Matplotlib

Skills: SQL analytics, data warehousing, window functions, return and volatility analysis, financial reporting, data visualisation

Australian Housing Data Quality Simulator

Developed a configurable Python tool that generates realistic synthetic Australian residential property data for data-cleaning, ETL, SQL, BI and machine-learning practice. The simulator covers every state and territory from 2020 to 2025 and models changing market conditions such as cash rates, auction clearance, buyer sentiment, lockdown effects and regional activity.

The output intentionally includes 15 categories of common data-quality problems, including missing values, duplicate records, mixed date and price formats, inconsistent categories, postcode mismatches, outliers and field swaps. This provides a reproducible and privacy-safe test environment for profiling, validation, transformation and downstream analytics workflows.

Technologies: Python, Pandas, NumPy, CSV

Skills: Synthetic data generation, data quality engineering, data modelling, ETL testing, validation design, analytics preparation
