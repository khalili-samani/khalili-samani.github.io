About Me

I am a data analyst with an MPhil in Mathematics from the University of Melbourne and practical experience using SQL, Python and Power BI to analyse data, develop dashboards and communicate findings clearly.

My background in mathematical modelling, statistics and quantitative analysis has developed a rigorous approach to problem-solving. Through academic and independent projects, I have worked across the analytics lifecycle, including database design, data extraction and transformation, data cleaning and validation, exploratory analysis, statistical modelling, dashboard development and reporting automation.

My projects have examined datasets from retail, financial markets, the Australian National Electricity Market and the Australian housing sector. This work has involved identifying trends, evaluating performance, improving the accessibility of reporting and translating analytical results into practical recommendations.

My core technical skills include SQL, Python, Power BI, data visualisation and ETL. I have also worked with MySQL, DuckDB, Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, XGBoost, Streamlit and Git. I focus on producing analysis that is accurate, reproducible and understandable to both technical and non-technical audiences.
