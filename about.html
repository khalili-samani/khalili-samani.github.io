About Me

I am a Quantitative Data Analyst with a Master of Philosophy in Mathematics from the University of Melbourne. My academic background developed strong analytical, statistical and problem-solving skills, which I now apply to building practical data-driven solutions.

My work focuses on transforming raw data into actionable insights through data engineering, SQL, statistical analysis, machine learning and interactive visualisation. I enjoy building complete analytical workflows, from collecting and cleaning data to developing predictive models and communicating insights through dashboards and reports.

Through independent projects, I have worked with Australian energy market data, financial market data and synthetic property datasets to develop solutions that reflect real-world analytics workflows. These projects include ETL pipelines, SQL reporting layers, forecasting models, AI-assisted reporting systems and dashboard applications.

I am currently seeking opportunities in Data Analytics, Quantitative Analytics and Analytics Engineering, where I can combine my mathematical background with modern data technologies to support evidence-based decision-making.
